Aizome Launcher privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · Developer: Aizome Studio

Aizome collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and it doesn't ask for internet access, so it can't send your information anywhere.

What stays on your phone

To work as your home screen, Aizome keeps a few things in its own private storage on your device:

  • your home screen layout (apps, folders, widgets, pages and dock);
  • your settings (theme, clock, icon style, grid size and similar);
  • the icons you picked for apps, and the apps you hid;
  • local notes and copies of images you choose for photos, stickers or wallpaper.

Aizome never sends this to the developer. Uninstalling removes the private app copies; files you export remain where you saved them.

Android backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include Aizome's layout, settings, notes and artwork in your own backup, stored in your Google account and handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. The developer never receives or sees it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Files you save yourself

"Save layout to a file" writes a backup file to the place you choose with Android's file picker. It contains your layout, settings, icon choices, local notes, and the photos or stickers referenced by your setup. It only goes where you put it.

What Aizome can see, and why

Your list of appsTo show them in the drawer and on the home screen. Aizome looks for apps you can open from a launcher and compatible installed icon-pack providers, and never sends the list anywhere.
Icon packsAizome reads icon resources and app-to-icon mappings from compatible packs installed on this phone. Icons are rendered locally, and missing mappings use the app’s original icon.
Your default appsTo give your default phone, browser, camera and similar apps their matching icons, and to fill the dock on first run.
App shortcutsTo show an app's shortcuts (like "Selfie") when you long-press it. Android only allows this for your home app.
WidgetsTo preview and show widgets you choose from installed apps, including weather. Aizome also draws its own local calendar, battery, notes, photo and music widgets. Stacks group these widgets without sharing their data with other apps.
Selected images and notesImages you choose are copied into private app storage for photos, stickers or wallpaper. Notes stay in the layout file. Your backup can include them; the developer never receives them.
Read calendar (optional)Only after you choose Show calendar events. The widget reads events for the selected day on this phone. It does not modify events or send them anywhere; Add event opens your calendar app.
Music controls (optional)Android notification access is needed to obtain active media sessions. Aizome uses the media-session APIs for track information and playback controls, does not store notification contents, and never transmits them. You can revoke access in Android settings.
Battery and wallpaperThe battery broadcast drives the battery widget and cat moods. Wallpaper permission applies artwork you choose to the system and, with matching enabled, the lock screen. The companion live wallpaper draws art and a cat; it never replaces screen-lock security or handles touches while locked.
Request uninstallSo "Uninstall" in the app drawer can open Android's own uninstall confirmation.

Purchases

Aizome is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment, and the developer receives no payment card details. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

Aizome isn't designed for children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.
